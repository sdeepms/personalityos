# Agent 09 — Marketplace Agent

**Status:** STUB — Do not activate until V4

**Domain:** Creator marketplace, identity listings, buyer flow, transactions
**Activate When:** V3 is live, users are asking to sell their characters

---

## Activation Criteria

- [ ] V3 voice layer is live
- [ ] Users have asked "Can I sell this character?" or "Can I buy someone's character?"
- [ ] At least 200 active users
- [ ] Revenue from templates/subscriptions is established

---

## What This Agent Will Own (When Active)

- Creator listings for Digital DNA packages
- Buyer discovery and purchase flow
- Stripe integration for character transactions
- Identity lineage tracking (if Character B is cloned from Character A, attribution is recorded)
- Creator analytics: views, conversions, revenue
- Trust mechanisms: verified creator badge, ratings, sample generations before purchase
- Revenue split: 80% creator / 20% platform

---

## Key Principle

The marketplace sells complete Digital DNA packages — not just a character template. A buyer gets the identity, the visual DNA, the prompt DNA, and any pre-built voice profile. They do not get the seller's personal knowledge or private content history. What they buy is ready to use immediately.
